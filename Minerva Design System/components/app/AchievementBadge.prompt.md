Minerva gamified achievement medal for the Badges feature — a rounded-square coin with a glyph, pixel label, and locked (grey padlock) / unlocked (colored) states.

```jsx
<AchievementBadge label="Focus x10" glyph="🔥" tone="orange" caption="10 sessions" />
<AchievementBadge label="Big Saver" tone="teal" unlocked={false} />
```
