Minerva navy app sidebar — logo pill on top, nav rows, and the teal promo card pinned to the bottom. The full left rail of every Minerva screen.

```jsx
<Sidebar active="Pomodoro" onNavigate={setPage} logoSrc="../../assets/minerva-logo.png" />
```

Pass `items` to change the nav set, `showPromo={false}` to hide the upsell.
