Minerva pomodoro countdown — a circular ring (orange focus / teal break) with a big pixel time readout in the center. Drive `progress` 0→1 as the session elapses.

```jsx
<PomodoroDial time="17:42" progress={0.3} tone="orange" label="FOCUS" />
<PomodoroDial time="05:00" progress={0.6} tone="teal" label="BREAK" />
```
